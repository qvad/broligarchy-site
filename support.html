Common questions

There is no sound. The game follows the ring/silent switch. Turn the switch to ring, and check that music and SFX are on in Settings.

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

The screen does not rotate to portrait. The game is played in landscape only.

The app was closed during a hearing. Open it again and play the hearing again. You keep your signal bars.
