BROLIGARCHY

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy covers the Broligarchy app for iPhone and iPad.

Data we collect

None. Broligarchy does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data.

Network

The game runs offline. It does not send anything over the network. The only exception is a link you tap yourself, such as the link to this page, which opens in Safari.

Data on your device

Your game progress and settings (sound, art style, campaign progress) are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They never leave the device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
