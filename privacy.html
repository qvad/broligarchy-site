BROLIGARCHY

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy covers the Broligarchy app for iPhone and iPad.

Data we collect

None. Broligarchy does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data.

Network

The game runs offline. It sends nothing over the network, with two exceptions: a link you tap yourself, such as the link to this page, opens in Safari; and if you are signed in to Game Center, iOS sends your Broligarchy scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. Your scores and achievements are kept by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Apple does not pass them to us, and we receive no personal data from Game Center.

Data on your device

Your game progress and settings (sound, art style, campaign progress) are saved on your device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes this data. Scores and achievements already sent to Game Center stay in your Game Center account.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
